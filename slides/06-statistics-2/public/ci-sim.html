The true mean $\mu$ is fixed at 0 (the pink dashed line). Each black row is one realised 95% CI built from its own random sample of size $n$. The intervals that miss $\mu$ are coloured pink. The 95% is a property of the procedure across many samples — for any single interval, it either contains $\mu$ or it does not.
