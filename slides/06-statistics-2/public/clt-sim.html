Central limit theorem

Draw many samples of size n from a source distribution, compute the standardised mean for each, and plot the histogram. Compare with the standard Normal density. As n grows, the histogram approaches N(0, 1) — for any source with finite variance.

Cauchy has no finite variance, so the standardised mean does not converge to N(0,1) at any n. This is the canonical case where CLT fails.
