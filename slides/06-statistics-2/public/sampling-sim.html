From sample to sampling distribution

Each draw produces a fresh sample of $n$ observations. We compute one statistic from each — here the sample mean $\bar X = \frac{1}{n}\sum X_i$ — and drop that one number into the histogram below. As we accumulate draws, the histogram fills out the sampling distribution of $\bar X$.
