Power under $H_1$

We fix a true effect size $\mu_1 - \mu_0$ and simulate the two-distribution picture that ties $\alpha$ and $\beta$ together. The top panel shows $\bar X$ under $H_0 \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_0, \sigma^2/n)$ and under $H_1 \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_1, \sigma^2/n)$ with the shaded $\alpha$, $\beta$, and power regions. The bottom panel runs $M$ independent experiments under $H_1$ at the chosen effect, computes $Z$ each time, and tracks the empirical rejection rate. That number is the empirical power, equivalently the true positive rate. As $M$ grows it converges to the theoretical area in the top panel.
