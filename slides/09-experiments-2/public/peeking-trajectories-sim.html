P-value trajectories on A/A experiments

Every trajectory below is an A/A experiment. Treatment and control are drawn from the same distribution, so there is no true effect. We recompute the p-value every day as the sample grows. Trajectories that crossed α = 0.05 on at least one day are shown in red. A team that stops on first significance would call each of those a win.

Reading the chart. The dashed line at p = 0.05 is the rejection threshold. Each line is one A/A experiment over time. Trajectories that touched the threshold even once are colored red.
