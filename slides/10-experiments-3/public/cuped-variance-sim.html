CUPED variance reduction

For each user we have a pre-experiment value $X$ and an in-experiment value $Y$ with correlation $\rho$. Two estimators of the average treatment effect: $\bar Y_T - \bar Y_C$ without CUPED, and the same on $Y_\text{cuped} = Y - \hat\theta (X - \bar X)$ with CUPED. We run many A/A replications and plot both empirical distributions of the estimator. Both centre at 0. The CUPED histogram is narrower by a factor of $\sqrt{1 - \rho^2}$.

data $X_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$, $Y_i = \rho X_i + \sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,\varepsilon_i$, $\varepsilon_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$. By construction $\text{Var}(Y) = 1$ and $\text{Corr}(X, Y) = \rho$
A/A treatment $=$ control. Both estimators have expectation $0$
prediction $\text{Var}(\bar Y_\text{cuped}) / \text{Var}(\bar Y) = 1 - \rho^2$
