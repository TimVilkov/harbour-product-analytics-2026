Type M: the winning tail of H₁

Two distributions of the estimate $\hat\Delta$. Under $H_0$ there is no effect, $\hat\Delta \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$, and the rejection threshold $\lvert\hat\Delta\rvert \gt 1.96$ is read off this curve (two-sided $\alpha = 5\%$). Under $H_1$ the true effect is $\Delta$, so $\hat\Delta \sim \mathcal{N}(\Delta, 1)$. Power is the share of the $H_1$ curve past the threshold, the pink tail. The average estimate inside that tail (dashed pink) sits to the right of $\Delta$: a win reports more than the truth.
