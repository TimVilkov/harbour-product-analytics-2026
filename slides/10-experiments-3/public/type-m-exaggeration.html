Type M: exaggeration vs power

The exaggeration ratio is how much a significant win overstates the truth, $\mathbb{E}[\lvert\hat\Delta\rvert \mid \text{reject}] / \Delta$. It depends only on power. At high power the curve sits near $1$: winners report close to the true effect. As power falls the curve climbs steeply, past $2\times$ below 20% power. The dot tracks the current setting.
